Study Case on European Pharmaceutical Market Analysis

-- Conclusion (Q3)
-- Findings:
-- Monthly trend by total sales:
--   Sales show a clear seasonal pattern within the year: winter months concentrate
--   the highest sales. January (seasonal index 126) and December (120) lead the
--   monthly average, with sales 26% and 20% above the average month, respectively.
--   Summer months concentrate the lowest sales. June (seasonal index 81), July (79) and
--   August (81) show sales around 20% below the average month.
--   Colder months in general have a higher seasonal index, with above average sales from October
--   to March, and below average from April to September.
-- Monthly trend by category:
--   N02BE and R03 accentuate the overall seasonal trend, with sales far below the average month
--   in June, July and August, and sales far above the average month in January and December.
--   For N02BE, January (seasonal index 138) and December (133) show sales 38% and 33% above
--   the average month, respectively. While June (67), July (66) and August (69) show sales
--   around 33% below the average month.
--   For R03, January (seasonal index 124) and December (144) show sales 24% and 44% above
--   the average month, respectively. while July (54) and August (57) show sales
--   around 46% and 43% below the average month, respectively.
--   R06 shows an opposite pattern, January (57) and December (48) show sales 43% and 52% below
--   the average month, respectively. while April (163), May (179) and June (153) show sales
--   63%, 79% and 53% above the average month, respectively.
